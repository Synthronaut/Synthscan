SYNTHSCAN
Your verse, connected.

One installer. Your choice of features.

All features start checked. On the next page, uncheck anything you do not want.
Live Mining Recognition is the only screen-reading add-on. Its label covers
rock signatures, detailed composition/quality readings and refinery imports.
Unchecked reader components are not installed.

Your privacy
Player records, ownership, notes and account credentials are not included in
this installer and are not shared with other machines. SynthScan does not
collect usage analytics or upload screen frames. Supported account features
use your locally signed-in game session; no password is requested.

Your game
Optional SynthStrings manages the English localization override and language
setting, with recovery copies in AppData. It does not patch the game executable
or archives. Setup explains any game-text permission request separately.

Your controls
Automatic Acceptance and the AFK helper stay off until you enable them. The
mining reader does not move your mouse or control your ship.

Alpha release
Star Citizen changes frequently. Source-dependent readings and community
timers can be unavailable or estimates; SynthScan labels these states. This
community application is not affiliated with Cloud Imperium Games.

Shared UI and game-data infrastructure remain in Core. Optional background
providers, feature resources and screen-reading dependencies follow your
selection. You can change features later from Feature modules in SynthScan.
